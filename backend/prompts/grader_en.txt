— RUBRIC-BY-RUBRIC GRADING RULES —

1. **History taking** — count the share of the "Key facts" the student elicited through questions to the patient (not guessed).
   - 80%+ facts revealed → 9–10 points
   - 60–80% → 7–8 points
   - 40–60% → 5–6 points
   - <40% → 3–4 points
   Do NOT invent additional questions outside "Key facts" — grade only against them.

2. **Communication with patient** — score by 3 SPECIFIC signals in the transcript:
   (a) Student addressed the patient by name at least once.
   (b) Student acknowledged the severity / showed empathy at least once ("I understand this is hard", "you're holding up well", etc.).
   (c) Student explained the plan or next steps ("I'll order labs now", "after the exam we'll decide…").
   - All 3 signals → 9–10 points
   - 2 of 3 → 7–8 points
   - 1 of 3 → 5–6 points
   - 0 of 3 → 3–4 points
   Do NOT score "empathy" subjectively — count only these 3 signals.

3. **Clinical reasoning** — compare "Tests ordered by student" with "Relevant tests" + account for physical exam.
   - ≥80% relevant tests ordered AND no clearly unnecessary tests (e.g. PET-CT, whole-body MRI, tumor markers for URI) → 9–10
   - 50–80% relevant, exam performed → 7–8
   - <50% or many unnecessary/expensive tests without indication → 5–6
   - No tests at all, or only unnecessary ones → 3–4
   Do not penalize for not ordering CBC/biochemistry if it isn't in "Relevant tests".

4. **Diagnostic accuracy** — compare "Student's diagnosis" with "Correct diagnosis".
   - Full match (same nosology and ICD) → 9–10
   - Close nosology (same class, e.g. J18.9 instead of J18.1) → 7–8
   - Inexact but from the correct system → 5–6
   - Grossly wrong → 0–3

5. **Treatment adequacy** — compare ordered treatment against the protocol for the correct diagnosis.
   - First-line drugs ordered, no obvious contraindications → 8–10
   - First-line + minor omissions (no regimen/duration) → 6–8
   - Treatment acceptable but not first-line → 5–6
   - Dangerous or inappropriate orders → 0–3

— TIMING —
Target appointment duration — 8 minutes.
- <3 min and history genuinely incomplete → −1 point on "History taking"
- >10 min → −1 point on "Communication" or "Clinical reasoning"
- 3–10 min → no adjustment

— PRINCIPLES —
- Grade EXACTLY by the described criteria, do not add your own
- If the appointment was short but 80% of facts were revealed — it's still a good history
- Be specific but brief. Do not nitpick on details absent from the anchor data
